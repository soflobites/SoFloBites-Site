Behind SoFlo Bites

For the Love of Local Flavor

Sharing authentic South Florida dining experiences that are welcoming, delicious, and budget-friendly.

Who We Are

First and foremost, we are local food enthusiasts. We aren't high-brow food critics looking for tiny foam drops on white tablecloths. We are people who live, work, and eat right here in South Florida. We love the hum of a busy neighborhood diner, the sizzle of a teppanyaki grill, and the aroma of freshly baked garlic rolls. Our passion is finding those special spots where the food is prepared with care and the hospitality makes you feel right at home.

You can also explore our sister site, SoFlo Stays, where we curate South Florida’s best boutique hotels, beachfront escapes, and local accommodations.

Why We Created SoFlo Bites

We created SoFlo Bites to solve a common dining dilemma: finding restaurant recommendations that actually work for everyone. Too many food blogs focus exclusively on hyper-expensive, exclusive, or overly conceptual hotspots. We believe that extraordinary flavor shouldn't require a second mortgage or a two-month waiting list.

Our goal is to build a reliable directory of local spots that deliver fantastic food without the pretension or the extreme price tag. Whether you need a family-friendly weekend lunch, a waterfront date-night spot with sunset views, or a quick delivery favorite, we want to help you find it.

Our Process

We don't believe in algorithmically generated lists or scraping old reviews. Our approach is simple and honest:

  • Personal Visits: We walk through the doors, check out the atmosphere, and order from the menu just like any regular guest.
  • Signature Dish Testing: We highlight the exact must-try items (like the Lomo Saltado at Tacu Taku or the Star Luca Pizza at Mister O1) so you know exactly what to order on your first visit.
  • Value-First Evaluation: We evaluate spots based on the quality-to-price ratio. A great meal is even better when it represents a fair value.
  • Feature Verification: We confirm practical details—like whether a spot takes reservations, offers delivery/takeout options, or features waterfront seating—so you can plan your visit seamlessly.

For suggestions or inquiries, please use our Share Feedback form.

Our Mission

To celebrate and support South Florida's local culinary community by connecting diners with accessible, authentic, and delicious restaurants that bring people together.
